What you'll see here

  1. Writer — the Omni model's LLM turns your source into a storyboard.
  2. Illustrator — its image model paints every scene; the LLM looks at each picture and asks for a redraw if it misses.
  3. Voice — its speech model narrates; the listener transcribes every line back and fixes words it mispronounced.
  4. Editor — scenes, captions and chapters are cut into an MP4, with a report of what ran where.
